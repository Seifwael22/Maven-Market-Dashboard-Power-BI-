# Maven Market Dashboard Analysis

## **Project Overview**

This project presents a detailed analysis of the **Maven Market dataset**, a dataset containing retail sales information. The goal of the analysis is to examine and present insights into the top-line performance of the business, specifically focusing on the years **1997** and **1998**. The analysis utilizes **Power BI** and **DAX** to build an interactive dashboard that helps identify business trends, evaluate performance, and make informed decisions.

### **Dataset Used**
The dataset used for this analysis is the **Maven Market dataset**, which includes sales data across various products, customers, stores, and regions.

### **Goal of the Analysis**
The primary goal of this project is to:
- Analyze **top-line performance** for 1997 and 1998.
- Understand key metrics such as **total revenue**, **total profit**, **sales trends**, and **customer behavior**.
- Present insights through a series of visualizations to aid in business decision-making.

## **Key Metrics & Insights**

### **KPIs & Metrics**

Several KPIs and metrics were focused on in this analysis, including:

- **Total Revenue**: Measures the total revenue generated by all transactions.
- **Total Profit**: Represents the total profit made from all transactions.
- **Return Rate**: The percentage of products that were returned by customers.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Represents the average amount spent per order.
- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: Measures how often customers make repeat purchases.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Indicates the total value a customer brings over their lifetime.

### **Main Findings**
- **Revenue Trends**: The analysis revealed fluctuations in revenue in both 1997 and 1998. Understanding these fluctuations helps identify peak sales periods and product performance.
- **Product Performance**: Some product brands performed significantly better than others. This information is vital for inventory management and marketing strategies.
- **Return Rates**: High return rates were observed for some products, indicating possible issues in customer satisfaction or product quality.
- **Customer Behavior**: Repeat purchases were concentrated in specific customer segments, suggesting potential areas for targeted marketing.

## **Data Cleaning & Transformation**

The dataset was cleaned and transformed in the following ways to ensure it was ready for analysis:

- **Standardized Data Formats**: Ensured that date, currency, and region-related columns were consistent across the dataset.
- **Created New Columns**: 
  - **Customer Table**: Created columns for full name, year of birth, and customer behavior (e.g., "Has Children").
  - **Product Table**: Added a discounted price column and ensured proper encoding for categorical data.
  - **Store Table**: Created a full address column and extracted the area code.
  - **Calendar Table**: Added additional columns for time-based analysis such as start of the week, day name, month name, quarter, and year.
- **Measures**:
  - **All Transactions**: Measures total transactions across time.
  - **AOV**: Calculates the average order value for all customers.
  - **Return Rate**: Measures the percentage of returns.
  - **Discounted Profit and Revenue**: Tracks revenue and profit with respect to discounts.
  - **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Calculates the potential future value of a customer.
